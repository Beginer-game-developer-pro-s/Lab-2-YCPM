Interview 1 - Patient (Jane Smith, 34 years old)
------------------------------------------------
"I often have to call many times before I can book an appointment because I
don't know which doctor is available. I want to be able to search doctors by
specialty and see open time slots on my phone. I also often forget my
appointments, so I really need reminders."

Interview 2 - Doctor (Dr. Robert Tran, General Medicine)
------------------------------------------------------
"I need to see a patient's visit history before they walk into the room, and
it's especially important that the system warns me if I prescribe something
that conflicts with a known allergy. There was a near-miss where I almost
prescribed the wrong drug because I couldn't recall the full history."

Interview 3 - Receptionist (Patricia Lee)
------------------------------------------------------
"Many patients book online and then don't show up, wasting the slot. I want
a confirmation step before the booking is finalized, and I need to see the
list of pending confirmations for the day."

Interview 4 - Hospital Administrator (David Nguyen)
------------------------------------------------------
"The board needs a report on visit volume by doctor and by department for
staffing plans. I also need to manage the doctor list and update working
schedules when a doctor takes leave."

Quick survey (50 patients, online form)
------------------------------------------------------
- 82% want reminder notifications via SMS/chat app.
- 65% have experienced calling but nobody answering the phone.
- 40% care about verifying health insurance coverage online.

Workshop with the hospital IT team
------------------------------------------------------
- The legacy system used to crash during the 8-9 AM peak hour.
- Medical record data is sensitive information; it must be encrypted and
  access strictly controlled per Ministry of Health regulations.
